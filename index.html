ABOUT

About me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Zoology, Banaras Hindu University, working under the supervision of Prof. Gyaneshwer Chaubey.

My research focuses on understanding population history, genetic diversity, demographic history and evolutionary relationships using whole-genome and mitochondrial DNA data.

I have a strong interest in computational approaches to population genomics, ancient DNA and evolutionary genetics.
