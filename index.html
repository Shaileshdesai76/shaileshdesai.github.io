About me

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Zoology at Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi, working under the supervision of Prof. Gyaneshwer Chaubey.

My research focuses on understanding genetic diversity, population structure, demographic history and evolutionary relationships using genome-wide and mitochondrial DNA data.

I am particularly interested in computational approaches that allow us to reconstruct population history from modern and ancient genomic data.

As I approach the completion of my PhD, I am interested in postdoctoral research opportunities and collaborations in population genomics, ancient DNA, evolutionary genetics and computational genomics.
