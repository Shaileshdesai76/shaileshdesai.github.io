About me

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Zoology at Banaras Hindu University, working with Prof. Gyaneshwer Chaubey.

My research is centered on population genetics and evolutionary genomics, with a particular interest in using DNA to understand human migration, population admixture, ancient societies and the origins and spread of agriculture.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between human history and the movement, domestication and evolution of animals. My current research interests include pigs, dogs, chickens, buffaloes and cattle.

My broader goal is to understand how domestication shaped animal gene pools through selection and introgression, and to investigate these processes across both spatial and temporal scales using modern and ancient samples.
